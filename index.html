Explore Nearby

Close To Shantiniketan's Most Loved Destinations

Stay connected to key cultural hubs, craft markets, and iconic landmarks of Tagore's legacy.

📍

Visva-Bharati Area

Easily accessible for visitors wishing to explore Rabindranath Tagore's historic university campus and ashram grounds.

📍

Sonajhuri Haat

A short drive away from the famous open-air Saturday crafts market set amidst serene Sonajhuri trees and Baul music.

📍

Prantik Area

Conveniently positioned near Prantik station and surrounding calm countryside hamlets.

📍

Local Cultural Attractions

Close to museums, art galleries, Rabindra Bhavana, and traditional handicrafts centers.

📍

Shopping & Markets

Nearby markets for authentic Kantha stitch embroidery, terracotta crafts, and wooden artifacts.

📍

Restaurants & Cafes

Surrounded by charming traditional Bengali eateries and cozy cafes serving local delicacies.
